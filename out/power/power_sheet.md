# Pit Bench 5 V Power: Build Sheet

Generated by `cad/power_calc.py` from `out/pit_meshes.json`. Plan drawing: `wiring_plan.svg`.

## System

- The walk-in gaps split the ring into a **west** and an **east** half of 5 benches. Each half has its own shared base and its own 5 V supply, so no base or cable crosses a walkway.
- Each half's base is built from one section per bench, bolted together at the seams and, at the corners, through two hardwood spacers into the end section's back rail.
- **3/4" PVC conduit** runs along the back of every base section. Slip couplings join it at the seams, and a short nipple and coupling take it into a **pull box** at each corner. Pull the cables once the base is assembled.
- Each supply sits outside the pit, behind its player end, and plugs into the wall. A short heavy feeder runs into a Powerpole inlet on the outer rail of the **power base**, under the middle end bench. The power base holds the **fuse block**: one fuse per run, protecting that bench's cable.
- **One unbroken cable per bench** runs from the fuse block through the conduit to an **outlet box** under that bench's left end. It leaves the base through a **cord grip** in the base top (strain relief), with about 300 mm of slack.
- The cable goes up through a 35 mm grommet in the bench bottom and up a birch chase in the back-left corner. It ends in a **GX16 4-pin aviation plug** (2 pins +5 V, 2 pins GND) that screws into the bench's **control box**. A P-clip holds the cable just before the plug, so pulling on the cable doesn't load the connector.
- The **control box** sits high on the back liner, under the lid. It holds the bench fuse, a 1000 µF capacitor, a terminal block (ferruled wires), the ESP32 and a 74AHCT125 level shifter for the LED data line. The LED feed leaves through cable glands and runs along the left end to the header, feeding both ends of the strip.
- To move a bench: open the lid, unscrew the GX16, feed the plug down through the grommet and lift the bench off its pins. The cable stays in the base.

## Notes

- Wire the GX16 the same way on every bench (pins 1+2 = +5 V, 3+4 = GND), so any bench fits any position.
- Fuses at both ends: the fuse block protects each cable, and the fuse in the control box protects the bench.
- To buy one wire size, use 14 AWG for every run. It meets the drop budget on all of them.
- Re-run with the real strip's W/m once it is chosen: `--strip-w-per-m`.
