# Pit Bench 5 V Power: Build Sheet

Generated by `cad/power_calc.py` from `out/pit_meshes.json`. Plan drawing: `wiring_plan.svg`.

## System

- The walk-in gaps split the ring into a **west** and an **east** half of 5 benches. Each half has its own 5 V supply, so no wiring crosses a walkway.
- Each supply sits outside the pit, behind its player end, and plugs into the wall. A short heavy feeder runs into a Powerpole inlet on the outer rail of the **power base**, under the middle end bench.
- The power base holds a fused distribution block. From it, **one separate run per bench** goes through the cable slots in the bases to that bench's base connector.
- Each bench drops onto its base. Two steel locating pins enter their bushings 13 mm before the Powerpole halves meet, so the pins align the connector and the bench's weight mates it.
- Inside the bench, the pair runs up the wire chase (hidden behind the left side border) into the LED header and feeds the strip's left end. A second pair runs back along the LED channel and feeds the right end.

## Notes

- The bench half of each Powerpole pair is fixed in the bench bottom. The base half floats in its bracket, so the pins do the aligning. Use the same red/black orientation everywhere so any bench fits any base.
- The base half is always live, so it is the shrouded side. Powerpole contacts sit back inside the housing, but keep the pocket clear of coins and screws. The per-run fuse limits the current if something does short it.
- To buy one wire size, use 14 AWG for every run. It meets the drop budget on all of them.
- Re-run with the real strip's W/m once it is chosen: `--strip-w-per-m`.
