# Display Lighting

## Intent

The lighting is for seeing the collection, not for glow. The light comes from the front and above, just behind the
front acrylic, and shines down onto the items. Nothing shines from the back.

## Design

A black **LED header** is glued behind the top rail of the display frame. A channel routed into the header's
underside holds an LED strip facing straight down. A flush opal acrylic diffuser covers the channel.

| Part | Dimensions |
|---|---|
| LED header | 816 × 38 × 44.5 mm, black ply, glued behind the frame rail and between the two stays |
| Channel | 1/2″ (12.7 mm) wide × 10 mm deep, routed into the header's underside, starting 3 mm behind the acrylic |
| LED strip | 802 mm, 10 mm wide strip, facing down |
| Diffuser | 1/8″ (3 mm) opal acrylic, 16.7 mm wide, flush in a 2 mm-per-side rebate over the channel |

The header also does two other jobs:

- **It holds the acrylic.** The front acrylic stops at the window's top edge and slides up under the header, which
  captures its top edge.
- **It backs the hinge screws.** The case-leaf screws go through the 18 mm frame rail into the header, so they
  have more to grip.

The header clears the stays and rope limiters, and the lid swing check still passes to 108°.

## Why a header and not a routed frame rail

The frame rail is only 18 mm thick, and the acrylic sits behind it. A channel in the rail's own bottom edge would put
the strip in front of the acrylic, where it's exposed and shines into the acrylic instead of onto the items. The
header puts the strip just behind the acrylic, inside the display.

This is still an open choice: see [open-items.md](open-items.md).

## End windows

The end windows don't have their own strips. Light from the front strip reaches them. If the end views look too dark
once it's built, a short strip in a header over each end window would fit the same way. Space there is tight
around the rope anchors.

## Removed in this revision

- The strip under the lid.
- The end-window strips.
- The point lights in the viewers, now replaced by downlights from the strip position.
